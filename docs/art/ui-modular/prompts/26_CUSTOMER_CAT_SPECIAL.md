```text

conflicts with the cat's colours; reflect that choice in filename and video prompt.
```

## Flow — somente após aprovação do still

```text
Animate only this approved quadruped cat walking in place to the right. Fixed
camera, constant scale and contact plane; coherent alternating four-paw contacts,
natural feline gait, stable markings, collar and bell. No rotation towards camera,
translation, sliding, floating accessories or cuts. Keep the input's uniform opaque
chroma colour and full safe margins throughout. Output one 16:9 MP4.
```
